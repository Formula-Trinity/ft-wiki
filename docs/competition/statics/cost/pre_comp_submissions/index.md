# Cost Report Documents

A significant part of [Cost & Manufacturing Judging](../index.md) takes place before the competition, through a set of required submissions covering the cost, manufacture and carbon footprint of the car.

The Cost Report Documents (CRD) consist of:

- [Cost & Emissions Explanation Report](./explanations_report.md)
- [Costed Carbonised Bill of Materials (CCBOM)](./CCBOM.md)
- [Supporting Materials](./supporting_docs.md)

---

## Selected Area

Early in the year, FSUK announces the area of the car that will be assessed in detail for Cost & Manufacturing. The required documentation should cover this area in extreme detail, as the specific components examined during judging are not revealed until the competition.

For FTX7, the selected area was **Engine & Tractive System**, corresponding to the [**Powertrain**](../../../../ftx7/powertrain/index.md) department.

---

## Submissions

The submissions are separate documents but they should be treated as parts of the same overall assessment. Information presented across the CCBOM, explanation report and drawings should be consistent and the team should be able to justify the assumptions and decisions contained within them.

Particular attention should be paid to:

- completeness and accuracy
- realistic cost estimates
- realistic carbon estimates
- clear sourcing and justification of values
- consistency between documents
- compliance with the submission requirements

**!!! warning: check the submission requirements !!!**  
Only submit the documents required by the current year's rules. FTX7 received a **5-point pre-event penalty** for submitting an additional fourth document outside the required submission set.
