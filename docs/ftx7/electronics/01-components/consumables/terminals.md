# Terminals

## Why do we need them?

Terminals form the connection between a wire and another component. Depending on the connection, these may include connector contacts, ring terminals or crimp splices. Most terminals are attached by crimping, where a crimping tool squeezes the metal barrel of the terminal tightly around the wire.

A well-selected and installed terminal provides a secure, low-resistance connection that can withstand vehicle vibration. A loose or exposed terminal can cause issues like electrical faults, increased resistance, overheating or short circuits. 

## FTX7 

Terminals were protected with heat shrink wherever possible to prevent accidental short circuits. Wire splices were made using insulated crimp splices and were then covered with additional heat shrink.
