# Dynamic Events

The dynamic events test the car's acceleration, cornering, handling, efficiency and reliability on track. A car must pass all six stages of [scrutineering](../scrut/index.md) before it can compete.

## Events

| Event | What it tests | Maximum points |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| **Skid Pad** | Lateral grip and steady-state cornering on a figure-of-eight course. | 75 |
| **Acceleration** | Straight-line acceleration from a standing start over 75 m. | 75 |
| **Sprint** | Overall handling and pace over a short course of corners, straights and slaloms. This is called Autocross in the rules. | 100 |
| **Endurance** | Reliability and sustained performance over approximately 22 km, with a driver change at the midpoint. | 250 |
| **Efficiency** | The energy or fuel used during the Endurance run. | 100 |

Endurance and Efficiency are run together, making Sunday worth up to 350 points. The Endurance running order is based on the times recorded in Sprint.

## Typical Running Order

The schedule can change each year, but the recent FSUK format has been:

- **Friday:** Practice running may be available for cars that have passed scrutineering.
- **Saturday:** Acceleration and Skid Pad, followed by Sprint.
- **Sunday:** Endurance and Efficiency.

Passing scrutineering early gives the team the best chance to practise and enter every event. Queues close at the times stated in the official timetable, and weather or other delays can reduce the available running time, so a team should not rely on getting through scrutineering at the last moment.

Check the [official event timetable](https://www.imeche.org/events/formula-student/team-information/event-timetable) and [current rules](https://www.imeche.org/events/formula-student/team-information/rules) before each competition.
