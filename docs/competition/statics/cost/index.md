# Cost & Manufacturing Event

Cost & Manufacturing Judging assesses how well the team understands the cost, manufacturing and carbon footprint of the car.

The team submits its costing and carbon documentation before the competition, then attends a judging session where judges examine selected parts of the car and question the team on the assumptions and methods used:

- [Cost Report Documents](./pre_comp_submissions/index.md)
- [In-Person Judging](./judging/index.md)

---

## How it Works

- A particular area of the car is selected before the competition for more detailed Cost & Manufacturing judging.
- The team prepares this area particularly well and ensures it is fully costed and carbonised.
- The two specific components the judges want to inspect are only revealed during the judging session.
- For **FTX7**, the selected area was **Engine & Tractive System**, corresponding to our [**Powertrain**](../../../ftx7/powertrain/index.md) department.
- The components selected during judging were the **Plenum & Restrictor** and **Fuel Tank**.

Up to **10 team members** can attend the judging session. Ideally, attendees should collectively be familiar with the selected department, the CCBOM, cost assumptions, carbon calculations and team-level financial decisions.

---

## What is Assessed?

The Cost & Manufacturing event is judged under four categories:

- Content & completeness of the BOM
- Realism of cost estimation
- Realism of carbon footprint estimation
- Cost understanding
