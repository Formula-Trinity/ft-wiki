## T6.2.1 — Brake-failure actuation {#t621}

**Rule:** [T6.2.1](../../../../rules/section-t/6-brake-system.md#t621)<br>
**Status:** Open

**FTX7 compliance approach:** The SDC includes a BOTS. Record the mounting and trigger geometry across the full adjustment range with the brakes team.

**Verification needed:** Attach the geometry review and witnessed actuation results for the relevant hydraulic failures and adjustments.

**Evidence / result:** Pending — add the evidence link, result, configuration/revision, reviewer and date.

## T6.2.2 — No driver reset {#t622}

**Rule:** [T6.2.2](../../../../rules/section-t/6-brake-system.md#t622)<br>
**Status:** Open

**FTX7 compliance approach:** The chain lists a pull reset; this alone does not prove that the driver cannot operate it.

**Verification needed:** Demonstrate repeated pedal actuation and attempted reset from the driving position.

**Evidence / result:** Pending — add the evidence link, result, configuration/revision, reviewer and date.
