# Tractive System Accumulator Container Hand Cart

### EV8.1 Tractive System Accumulator Container Hand Cart

##### EV8.1.1

> A hand cart(s) must be used for transporting the TSAC(s) around the competition site.

##### EV8.1.2

> The hand cart(s) must have at least four wheels.

##### EV8.1.3

> The hand cart(s) must have a brake that is normally on and only released if someone
> pushes the handle, or similar.

##### EV8.1.4

> The brake must be capable of safely stopping the fully loaded hand cart.

##### EV8.1.5

> The cart must be easy to move when the brake is released.

##### EV8.1.6

> The hand cart(s) must be able to carry the load of the TSAC(s).

##### EV8.1.7

> The hand cart must provide a vertical firewall (see T4.8) to protect anyone moving the
> hand cart. The firewall must be the same width as the hand cart, must appropriately
> protect the operator’s legs and body  and be at least 30 cm higher than the hand cart
> handle and the TSAC.  Any part of the firewall more than 1.3m above the ground must be
> transparent.

##### EV8.1.8

> The TSAC(s) must be mechanically fixed to the hand cart(s) to enable safe transportation.

##### EV8.1.9

> The TSAC(s) must not protrude outside the edges of the hand cart.

##### EV8.1.10

> The TSAC(s) must be protected from vibrations and shocks during normal operation of the
> cart(s), e.g. by using pneumatic tyres.

##### EV8.1.11

> The hand cart must have a label according to EV5.3.8 on its firewall no more than 1.3m
> above the ground.  The label must be clearly visible when the TSAC is on the hand cart.

##### EV8.1.12

> The overall floor space used by the fully loaded hand cart must not exceed 1200mm x
> 800mm. If the TSAC is larger than the allowed floor space, exceptions may be approved by
> the officials prior to the competition.
