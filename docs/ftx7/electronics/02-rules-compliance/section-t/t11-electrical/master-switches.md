## T11.2.1 — Switch construction {#t1121}

**Rule:** [T11.2.1](../../../../rules/section-t/11-electrical-components.md#t1121)<br>
**Status:** Open

**FTX7 compliance approach:** The documented rotary LVMS addresses switch type. Confirm that the main current path is switched directly and that the handle meets every requirement.

**Verification needed:** Attach the switch datasheet, handle measurement and continuity demonstration.

**Evidence / result:** Pending — add the evidence link, result, configuration/revision, reviewer and date.

## T11.2.2 — Location and access {#t1122}

**Rule:** [T11.2.2](../../../../rules/section-t/11-electrical-components.md#t1122)<br>
**Status:** Open

**FTX7 compliance approach:** Add the LVMS position to the vehicle layout, using the Percy template rather than an estimated shoulder height.

**Verification needed:** Attach a dimensioned installation photograph and external-access check.
