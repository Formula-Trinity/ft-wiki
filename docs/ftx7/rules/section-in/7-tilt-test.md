# Tilt Test

### IN7.1 Tilt Test Procedure

##### IN7.1.1

> The tilt test will be conducted with the tallest driver fully strapped in normal driving
> position.

##### IN7.1.2

> The tilt test will be conducted with all vehicle fluids at their maximum fill level.

##### IN7.1.3

> The vehicle will be placed upon the tilt table and to an angle of 60°. There must be no fluid
> leaks and all wheels must remain in contact with the tilt table surface.
