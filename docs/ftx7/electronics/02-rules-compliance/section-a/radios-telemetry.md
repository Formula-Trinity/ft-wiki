## A6.13.2 — Other radio frequencies/power {#a6132}

**Rule:** [A6.13.2](../../../rules/section-a/6-general-rules.md#a6132)<br>
**Status:** Conditional

**FTX7 compliance approach:** If relying on this provision for team radios, record the equipment and applicable licence; this does not override the telemetry restriction in A6.13.3.

**Verification needed:** Attach the licence and configuration, or record no such use.

**Evidence / result:** Pending — add the evidence link, result, configuration/revision, reviewer and date.

## A6.13.3 — Telemetry frequency {#a6133}

**Rule:** [A6.13.3](../../../rules/section-a/6-general-rules.md#a6133)<br>
**Status:** Conditional

**FTX7 compliance approach:** Audit the Raspberry Pi and any telemetry equipment. The documented USB link does not prove wireless telemetry is disabled.

**Verification needed:** Attach the wireless configuration and compare it with the linked rule, or record that no wireless telemetry is transmitted.

**Evidence / result:** Pending — add the evidence link, result, configuration/revision, reviewer and date.
