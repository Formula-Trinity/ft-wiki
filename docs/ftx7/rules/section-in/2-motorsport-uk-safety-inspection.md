# Motorsport UK Safety Inspection

### IN2.1 Motorsport UK Safety Inspection Objective

##### IN2.1.1

> At the Motorsport UK Safety Inspection the driver’s equipment and various aspects of the
> vehicle are checked for compliance with the rules.

### IN2.2 Motorsport UK Safety Inspection Required Items

##### IN2.2.1

> The following items must be presented:
> - All helmets,
> - All driver’s equipment and other safety gear,
> - Two unused and in date fire extinguishers,
> - The vehicle.
