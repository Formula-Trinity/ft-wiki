# Thermal Tape

## Why do we need it?

Thermal tape is used to hold the individual wires together and provide an additional layer of protection.

It helps protect the harness from high temperatures, accidental cuts, abrasion, pinching or any other damage. 

The tape also keeps the harness compact, making it easier to route and secure inside the car.

## FTX7

On FTX6 the wiring was fairly exposed, with the wires not wrapped or heat shrunk at all. For FTX7, the completed wiring harness was wrapped in protective thermal tape rated for temperatures of up to 150 °C.

Heat protection is particularly important where the harness is routed near the engine and exhaust. The tape also keeps the wires together and protects them from pinching or accidental slicing.

Thermal tape provides additional protection, but it does not replace the insulation on the individual wires, heat shrink around terminals or the grommets, cable glands or connectors needed where wiring passes through the firewall.
