# LV Hybrid System

### CV5.1 LV Hybrid System General

##### CV5.1.1

> LV Hybrid System – The Hybrid Storage Container, motor(s) and all parts electrically
> connected to them.

##### CV5.1.2

> The LV hybrid system must be a LVS, T11.1 and T11.7 are applicable for all LV hybrid
> system components.

##### CV5.1.3

> All electrical parts of the LV hybrid system, except for ground terminals, must be protected
> to a minimum of IPxxB when energised.

##### CV5.1.4

> The Hybrid Storage Container (HSC) is defined as the electrical energy storage system,
> including the AIR and overcurrent protection that is used in the LV hybrid system.

##### CV5.1.5

> Transferring energy to the HSC from any other electrical storage system is prohibited during
> any dynamic event.

##### CV5.1.6

> A firewall, see T4.8, must be positioned between the HSC and the fuel tank.

##### CV5.1.7

> The HSC must be positioned according to T11.7.2, all other LV hybrid system components
> must be positioned within the surface envelope, see T1.1.18.

##### CV5.1.8

> The high current path of the LV hybrid system must meet EV4.5.16.

##### CV5.1.9

> LV hybrid motors must meet EV2.1 and EV2.2.3.

##### CV5.1.10

> The LV hybrid system may only be activated when the combustion engine is running or
> during engine start.

##### CV5.1.11

> The organisers may require the fitment of an Energy Meter, see EV4.6. The fuel efficiency
> of any LV hybrid systems will be calculated on a State of Charge (SOC) neutrality basis.

### CV5.2 Hybrid Storage Container

##### CV5.2.1

> The HSC must be attached to the Primary Structure, see T1.1.12 and meet T9.2.1.

##### CV5.2.2

> A disconnection mechanism, designed as an AIR, must be integrated inside the HSC,
> disconnecting the positive pole of the HSC. The AIR must be compliant with EV5.6.3.

##### CV5.2.3

> The maximum weight of all elements in the hybrid system that store electrical energy (e.g.
> battery cells or supercapacitors), including all casings and tabs integral to them, is 3 kg.

##### CV5.2.4

> Holes in the HSC (both internal and external) are only allowed for the wiring harness,
> ventilation, cooling or fasteners. At least 75% of the area of any single wall must remain.

##### CV5.2.5

> The HSC must be removable to be inspected at Technical Inspection and it must be possible
> to easily check the weight.

### CV5.3 LV Hybrid System Form

##### CV5.3.1

> A LV Hybrid System Form (HSF) must be submitted by the date published in the Key Dates
> document using the HSF template.

##### CV5.3.2

> The HSF must detail how the system design safely mitigates the risk of the LV hybrid
> system attempting to accelerate the car from rest, when the accelerator pedal is fully
> released. An APPS as per T11.8 is an acceptable way to mitigate this risk.

##### CV5.3.3

> If no HSF is submitted, or the HSF is not approved, the HSC must not be fitted to the vehicle
> at the competition.
