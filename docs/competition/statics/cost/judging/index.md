# Cost & Manufacturing Judging

The Cost & Manufacturing judging session assesses the team's understanding of the [submitted documentation](../pre_comp_submissions/index.md) and its ability to justify the cost, carbon and manufacturing decisions made.

Up to **10 team members** can attend the session. The exact format and number of judges may vary between years.

---

## What to Bring

- A printed copy of the CCBOM for reference during discussion.
- A searchable copy (*using* `ctrl+F`) of the CCBOM on a laptop so selected components can be found quickly.
- Two full printed sets of drawings so multiple judges can inspect them at the same time.
- Assembly drawings and fully dimensioned manufacturing drawings for the selected area.

The drawings and CCBOM should work as a complete manufacturing package: together, they should contain enough info for someone outside the team to make and assemble the relevant components.

---

## Judging Format

### Introductory Questions

Judging began with some general questions about how cost is managed within the team. For FTX7, this included questions such as:

- What is the team's overall budget?
- Are individual department budgets set?
- Who is responsible for cost within the team?
- Does the team have a dedicated Cost Lead?

FTX7 did not have individual department budgets or a designated Cost Lead.

Before judging, prepare the overall budget, actual expenditure and a clear explanation of how spending is approved and monitored. If the team does not allocate departmental budgets (*we didn't for FTX7*), be ready to explain the alternative process and identify who has responsibility for final spending decisions.

### Selected Components

Before competition, the team is informed of the area of the car which will receive detailed Cost & Manufacturing assessment. However, the specific components examined are only revealed during the judging session.

For FTX7, the selected area was **Engine & Tractive System**. During judging, the judges selected:

- **Plenum & Restrictor**
- **Fuel Tank**

The team was then asked to locate these components within the [**CCBOM**](../pre_comp_submissions/CCBOM.md) and walk through their contents.

### CCBOM Walkthrough

The judges examined the selected components in detail, checking the completeness and accuracy of the CCBOM.

This included areas such as:

- components and materials
- fasteners
- manufacturing processes
- assembly processes
- material, process and tooling order
- jigs and fixtures
- cost estimates
- carbon estimates

The team should be prepared to explain how the component would actually be manufactured and assembled, rather than treating the CCBOM as simply a list of parts and values.

### Wider Discussion

Questioning then expanded beyond the selected components into the assumptions and methods used throughout the submissions.

Topics included:

- labour rates
- indirect labour and overheads
- material costs
- cost drivers
- carbon calculations
- sourcing and transport assumptions
- information within the [**Cost & Emissions Explanation Report**](../pre_comp_submissions/explanations_report.md)
- submitted manufacturing drawings

The exact direction of the discussion will depend on the judging panel and the answers given by the team.

## FTX7 Feedback

### Content & completeness of BOM

Score: `15/20`
Penalty Points: `-3`
Awarded Points: `12/20`

```text
CBOM was accurate and included most items. All parts were
covered and in good detail. Jigs & Fixtures included.

Missing Assembly of plenum to restrictor & couple processes
wrong way around
```

### Realism of Cost Estimation

Score: `20/30`

```text
Cost estimations were good. Labour rates were well thought out with different rates included for
different skilled labour.

Need to break out indirect labour but had fringe benefits. Overheads lacking most cost drivers
apart from utilities and consumables - When quizzed managed as a team to come up with capital,
depreciation, floor space, maintenance, consumables & Utilities
```

### Realism of Carbon Foot print Estimation

Score: `8/15`

```text
They had carbon footprint explanation in a separate document. They had references of the
sourcing across every part in the DBOM.

Justification around carbon was poor and good be improved, including the main areas of the
cradle to gate process - transport is it all plane or some shipped?
```

### Cost Understanding

Score: `25/40`

```text
They had a moderate understanding of the cost drivers, needed a lot of coaching and guidance on
overheads, SG&A & Profit. They had very good understanding material. Very good CBOM
submitted, accurate, realistic costing, very detailed analysis
```

### Final Comments

Total Score: `63/115`
*(includes 5 pentalty points for extra document and 3 penalty points for missing items in CCBOM)*

```text
Very good CCBOM submitted. Good attitude by the team to a guidance and questioning. They all

BOM - Ok level of detail , tooling and jigs mentioned
CEEF - Labour rates with extras included, Overheads, cannot see machine depreciation

SMF - good level of detailed drawings
```
