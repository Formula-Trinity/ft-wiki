# Brake Test

### IN11.1 Brake Test Procedure

##### IN11.1.1

> Lock all four wheels and stop the vehicle in a straight line at the end of an acceleration run
> specified by the officials.
>
> Whilst at some point during the run all 4 wheels must be locked at the same time, it is
> acceptable for the front wheels to lock before the rear wheels.

##### IN11.1.2

> **[EV ONLY]** After accelerating, the tractive system must be switched off by the driver, using
> the cockpit-mounted shutdown button and the driver must brake using only the
> mechanical brakes. It is acceptable for the TSAL to switch to green shortly after the vehicle
> has come to a complete stop as the reduction of the system voltage may take up to 5s.

##### IN11.1.3

> The brake light and TSAL illumination will be checked, and the officials will verify if the
> illumination is satisfactory for external observation.
